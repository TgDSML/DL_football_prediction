Raw Plus Rolling Sequence Results
=================================

Feature Mode
============

raw_plus_rolling creates:

- 80 features per team timestep
- 160 concatenated home_away features per timestep

Shapes
======

home_away seq10 raw_plus_rolling:

- train: X=(6154, 10, 160)
- val: X=(1500, 10, 160)
- test: X=(1860, 10, 160)

home_away seq50 raw_plus_rolling:

- train: X=(5014, 50, 160)
- val: X=(1400, 50, 160)
- test: X=(1753, 50, 160)

Answers
=======

1. How many features per timestep does raw_plus_rolling create?

80 per team timestep and 160 for concatenated home_away tensors.

2. Does adding engineered rolling features improve accuracy?

No. The best raw_plus_rolling accuracy is 0.4855, below the best raw sequence
accuracy of 0.5204 and below the fixture-level classical ML accuracy of 0.5211.

3. Does it improve macro F1?

Not versus the best raw recurrent models. The best raw_plus_rolling macro F1 is
0.4519, below the best raw GRU macro F1 of 0.4549. It is slightly above the
best raw weighted RNN macro F1 of 0.4452 and above the classical fixture-level
macro F1 of 0.4237.

4. Does it beat the best raw-sequence RNN accuracy 0.5204?

No.

5. Does it beat the best raw-sequence GRU macro F1 0.4549?

No. The closest result is the regularized augmented GRU at 0.4519.

6. Does it beat or approach the classical ML baseline?

It beats the classical fixture-level macro F1 reference of 0.4237, but it does
not approach the classical accuracy reference of 0.5211.

7. Is the neural bottleneck mainly architecture or feature representation?

The augmented features alone do not solve the bottleneck. They increase input
dimensionality substantially and appear to make overfitting easier. The
regularized GRU nearly matches the best raw GRU macro F1, but accuracy remains
well below the best raw RNN and classical baselines.

Recommendation
==============

Do not replace the raw sequence baseline with raw_plus_rolling yet. The feature
representation is richer, but the current recurrent architectures do not use it
well enough without overfitting.

Proceed to scratch LSTM while keeping raw_plus_rolling available as an optional
feature mode for controlled LSTM comparisons.
